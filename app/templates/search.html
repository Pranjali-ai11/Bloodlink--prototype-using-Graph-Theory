Available Donors

Donors are ranked using the matching score, which considers urgency, donor reliability, and distance.

How donor ranking works
  • Urgency → higher urgency increases priority.
  • Reliability → more reliable donors receive a higher contribution.
  • Distance → closer donors receive a better contribution.
  • Final Match Score → donors are sorted from highest score to lowest score.
