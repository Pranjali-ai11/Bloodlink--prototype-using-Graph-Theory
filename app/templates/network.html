How the graph works
  • Blood-group edges point from donor group to compatible recipient group and reuse BloodLink's existing rules.
  • BFS discovers available and eligible donors in O(V + E).
  • Dijkstra uses weighted location edges and a min-heap in O((V + E) log V).
  • Find Donors and Network use the same backend rank, normalized match score and Dijkstra distance.
